About the Application

What is JNJC Boarding House Pay?

JNJC Boarding House Pay is a boarding house management and payment system designed to make boarding-related transactions easier, more organized, and convenient for tenants, landlords, and administrators.

What the App Provides

The system provides features for account management, boarding house information, room management, payment monitoring, payment history, notifications, and communication between users.
